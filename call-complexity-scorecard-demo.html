Scoring Methodology & Weight Rationale

Each contact scored across 6 KPI dimensions. Rated into Bucket 1 (low), 2 (medium), or 3 (high). Final score uses min–max normalization:

composite = Σ (bucket_scorei × weighti)

score = ((composite − 10.0) / 20.0) × 100

Min=10.0 (all Bucket 1) → Score 0  |  Max=30.0 (all Bucket 3) → Score 100

Low (0–25): Routine

Medium (26–40): Moderate effort

High (41+): Senior agent recommended
